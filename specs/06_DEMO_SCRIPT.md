# Miyo Demo Script

This script defines the ideal 2–3 minute product walkthrough. The app should make this sequence effortless.

## Scene 1 — Landing page (15 seconds)

Show headline:

> **Only what matters. When it matters.**

Briefly point out Gmail, Outlook, and Slack as connected sources.

Click **Try the demo**.

## Scene 2 — Dashboard (20 seconds)

Dashboard says:

> Good morning, Alex.
> You have 3 things that need you today. The rest can wait.

Show:

- 2 Urgent
- 5 Important
- 7 Needs Reply
- 31 safely deferred

Explain that Miyo does not merely combine inboxes; it decides what deserves attention.

## Scene 3 — Catch Me Up (30 seconds)

Click **Catch Me Up**.

Mascot performs a short sorting animation.

Reveal:

### What changed
- Project Atlas demo moved to 2:30 PM
- Client approved Concept B

### What needs you
- Approve Project Atlas pricing by 1:30 PM
- Answer Jordan's launch question
- Confirm tomorrow's research meeting

### Safe to ignore
- 11 newsletters
- 7 FYIs
- 5 casual messages

Finish with:

> **You’re caught up.**

## Scene 4 — Cross-platform Project Atlas (30 seconds)

Open Project Atlas.

Make it immediately visible that the project contains:

- Gmail
- Outlook
- Slack

Show chronological activity from all three.

Point out the unified summary:

> The client approved the revised concept. Tomorrow’s demo moved to 2:30 PM, and pricing approval is still waiting on you.

Show decisions and open questions.

## Scene 5 — Commitments (25 seconds)

Open Commitments.

Show **I Owe**:

- Approve Project Atlas pricing — today 1:30 PM
- Send investor deck — today 5:00 PM

Show **Owed to Me**:

- Sarah owes Q3 numbers — overdue
- Maya owes revised deck — noon

Mark one commitment complete and show a subtle confirmation.

## Scene 6 — Focus Mode + mascot moment (35 seconds)

Open Focus.

Select **Deep Work — 2 hours**.

Settings:

- Allow Urgent: ON
- Allow VIP: ON
- Auto Reply: ON

Start Focus Mode.

Show calm focus state:

> Miyo is watching your messages.
> I’ll only interrupt you if something actually needs you.

Click **Simulate urgent message**.

The mascot runs in carrying/pulling the urgent notification:

> Maya Chen · Slack
> Client moved the Project Atlas demo to 2:30 PM. Can you confirm you’re available in the next 20 minutes?

Miyo summary:

> **Demo moved to 2:30 PM. Maya needs your confirmation soon.**

This should be the emotional high point of the demo.

## Scene 7 — Close (10 seconds)

Return to dashboard or show a final calm state.

Copy:

> **Only what matters. When it matters.**

Optional closing line:

> Miyo turns communication overload into a clear plan for your attention.
